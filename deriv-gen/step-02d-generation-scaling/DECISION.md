**1000 raw proposals per seed**.

unique accepted identities, 19.47 one-cut MMPs,
and 37.70 novel useful-local analogues per
seed. The candidate population remains broader than a strictly local analogue space,
as shown by median seed similarity 0.460
and scaffold-retention fraction
12.53%.

was performed.
